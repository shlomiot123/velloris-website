Work at Velloris

Velloris is a consulting firm helping organizations embed AI and automation at the heart of their work. As an automation developer with us, you'll enjoy an easy mix of working from home and spending time on-site with clients, alongside a team of strong, experienced consultants who are always happy to help. It's a warm, supportive, flexible environment — exactly what you need to do your best work.

Open Positions

1–2 days a week

About the role

We're looking for a strong, experienced automation developer for a role that's mostly hands on. The core stack: a self-hosted n8n installation on Docker with Postgres, API integrations with enterprise systems, and an LLM layer with structured output and human approval. The work starts in the field — talking with managers and the teams themselves, understanding a business and operational pain point, translating it into a spec, and above all, building the automations yourself.

What you'll actually do

Design end-to-end data flow, from scattered sources into one organized data layer

Decide where the line falls between deterministic logic and a model's judgment

Own the production layer itself: separate environments, testing, monitoring, an audit log, and failure recovery

Work directly with the field — managers and teams — understanding the pain point, spec'ing it, and building an automation that actually works

Requirements

At least 3 years of experience building automations that run in production at real organizations

Deep command of n8n or Make, including dropping into code (JavaScript or Python)

Hands-on experience with enterprise system APIs: permissions, rate limits, failures, idempotency

Engineering-grade integration of LLMs: structured output, cost control, quality measurement, and falling back to deterministic logic

Comfortable with Git and a Linux/Docker server environment: deploying, updating, and maintaining self-hosted services

Fluent Hebrew, spoken and written, for direct conversations with clients and their teams

Comfortable writing SQL to query and shape data in Postgres or similar databases

Experience working directly with external clients or stakeholders, as a consultant or freelancer

Nice to have

AI processes in Hebrew, familiarity with Israeli systems (Priority, Hashavshevet, and similar), self hosted environments, RAG in production.

This role is open to candidates of all genders.
